## Generation prompts

### Concept 1

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: preview-only logo direction for Kainbu, a thoughtful self-hosted project management workspace with boards, notes and optional AI. Design with the restraint and craft of an excellent independent identity studio. This is exploratory branding, not a website redesign.
Presentation: one landscape identity artboard, plain warm off-white #f9f9f6 background. Large beautifully balanced logo lockup in the central area, abundant whitespace. A tiny concept number/name at upper left. At lower right show the same symbol at favicon size in dark ink, and a small dark ink square containing its off-white reverse. All elements flat and crisp, vector-friendly silhouettes. No gradients, shadows, 3D, texture, mockups, photos, decorative layouts, AI sparkles, checkmarks or stock productivity icons. No additional slogans. Brand spelling exactly as specified.
Concept label: "01 / CUT"
Create an original compact architectural capital K monogram. A substantial vertical stem and two exact diagonal arms built from just three confident solid shapes. A carefully cut, slightly offset negative-space channel where the arms meet makes it distinctive; do not just typeset a K. Soft optical corrections but predominantly square terminals. The silhouette should read as K at 16 pixels and feel quiet, balanced, proprietary. Use muted terracotta #a3482c for the large symbol. Alongside it, exact text "Kainbu" in dark olive-black #262820, a refined neutral grotesk wordmark with excellent custom kerning, moderate weight, not bold. Small examples use the symbol alone.
